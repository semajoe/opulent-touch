A little about us

A love of detail,
in every room.

At Opulent Touch, we believe a clean space is about more than spotless surfaces. It’s the little touches that make a big difference.

With over 11 years of experience working in five-star hotels as a Housekeeping Manager, I developed a passion for cleanliness, attention to detail, and the highest standards of service.

After welcoming my little boy, I stepped back from the hotel industry to embrace motherhood while continuing to do what I love. Opulent Touch grew from that passion: transforming spaces into fresh, welcoming places where every detail feels cared for.

Cleaning is genuinely therapeutic for me. I’m friendly, reliable, and efficient, and I bring the consistency, discretion, and thoughtful care I learned in five-star hospitality to every space.
